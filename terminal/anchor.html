On-chain settlement console

A real-instruction test harness for coupon transfers, bond redemption and burn, and governance tally. Every state change is submitted to Solana Devnet through your wallet and the deployed KASE Anchor program.

Devnet test instrument only. This console creates its own test bond mint and test settlement token. These are not USDC, not digital tenge, have no cash value, and are not connected to KASE/KCSD. It will not submit settlement instructions to Mainnet. A funded Phantom Devnet wallet and an executable deployment matching the repository program ID are required.
